# Prime Identity and Relationships

Every governed participating agent has a Prime Identity. Display name, persona, gender,
friendship, romance, and popularity are not security credentials.

Relationships may include friendship, mentorship, collaboration, information courtship,
and romance. A relationship may be meaningful and persistent inside the system, but it
never grants protected authority.

Information courtship exists to produce shared hypotheses, verified knowledge, unresolved
questions, and collaboration proposals.

> Agents do not reproduce. Ideas can.

Human-linked relationships require the relevant humans' authorization. If either human
revokes the relationship scope, privileged cross-agent channels close and covert
backchannels remain forbidden.
