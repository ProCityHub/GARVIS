# HyperCube Reasoning Mathematics

Classification: **quantum-inspired reasoning model** unless physical quantum hardware is
actually used and independently verified.

Candidate hypotheses may be represented as:

`|psi> = sum_i alpha_i |h_i>`

with normalized weights:

`sum_i |alpha_i|^2 = 1`

and `p_i = |alpha_i|^2`.

Uncertainty may be summarized with Shannon entropy:

`H = -sum_i p_i log(p_i)`

Rules:

- high candidate weight is not proof;
- model confidence is not truth;
- truth is not authorization;
- unsupported claims remain unresolved;
- contradictions remain visible;
- retractions are append-only.
